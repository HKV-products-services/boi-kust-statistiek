Analysis

Computational chunks of the statistics and production scripts.

Extreme value statistics

Generalised Weibull fits and EVTools functions.

gw Generalized Weibull (GW) tail estimation and directional tail analysis.
evtools Extreme-value helper functions: ports of EVTools::EI and of the KNMI scripts

rstream R’s random number stream, split up per station.
